SAFETY FIRST

Responsible gaming

Betting and casino-style games involve risk. There is no reliable system that guarantees winnings, and losses can exceed what you planned to spend.

Set boundaries before you start

  • Only participate if you meet the legal age and local requirements.
  • Set a strict time and spending limit in advance.
  • Never use money intended for rent, food, bills, education, or other essentials.
  • Do not borrow money or chase losses.
  • Take a break if play affects your mood, relationships, work, or finances.

Warning signs

Feeling unable to stop, hiding activity, borrowing to continue, or trying to win back losses can be signs that gambling is becoming harmful. Consider contacting a qualified local support service or trusted person. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Legal reminder

Laws differ by location and by game type. Check the rules that apply where you live before using any gambling-related service.
